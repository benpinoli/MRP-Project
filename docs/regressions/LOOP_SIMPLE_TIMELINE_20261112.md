**What stands:** we have historical prediction gains in some samples, not zero predictability. Novo rolling level OLS reduced MSE against the rolling mean of past **changes** by about **44% at two years and 21% at five years** under pragmatic coverage. This remains retrospective evidence.

**What changed:** LH5 introduced the mean **terminal level**, minus today's discount. The new batch now tests the corresponding rolling mean too. Novo level/state models lose to matching anchors; its limited five-year expanding activity exception remains visible. The benchmarks, training policies and exceptions must stay distinct.

**What remains open:** economic mechanisms and execution are not certified by a forecast win; unavailable data are not negative mechanism results. The [paper's complete tree appendix](LOOP_HYPOTHESIS_TESTS_PAPER_20261112.md#7-every-node-in-the-hypothesis-tree) distinguishes tested descriptions/proxies from untested or data-gated hypotheses. Existing verdicts and results are unchanged. The [active plan](LOOP_ACTIVE_RESEARCH_PLAN_20261112.md) remains the operational queue.
