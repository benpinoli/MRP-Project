# Bounded payout-delivery and Novo split/conversion studies

Authorized November 12, 2026 after the paper's readiness review. This specification fixes two descriptive studies. Existing prices, source reports and some outcome values have already been inspected; this is not an unseen-outcome preregistration. No regression, event-window search, forecast optimization or causal verdict is authorized by this protocol.

## A. Samsung payout commitments versus reported delivery — P05

Review the dated October 2015 policy, its November 2016 enhancement, October 2017 policy for FY2018–2020 and the targeted January 2018 clarification of FY2017 dividends. Reuse already acquired business reports for FY2015–2020, with FY2021 used only to check later confirmation of FY2020 delivery. Do not collect generic annual reports.

Transcribe each fiscal year's reported aggregate cash dividend and common/preferred annual DPS from its own report page, retaining that report's native unit, approval/proposal caveat and unknown original publication timestamp. Normalize pre-2018 DPS by the documented 50-for-1 split only in a separately labeled comparison column. Retain later comparative restatements as separate source-vintage checks, not extra observations.

Compute arithmetic totals, year-to-year reported dividend changes, the class cash difference and whether reported amounts are consistent with the announced rounded annual dividend amounts. Preserve the change from the initially announced FY2017 dividend to the later final proposal: a doubling claim must use its actual announcement-time base. Separate FY2020 regular and special components where supported; do not compare total including special to a regular-only commitment without that distinction.

Free-cash-flow compliance is not established by dividends alone. Retain issuer retrospective claims separately from an independently reconciled policy-defined FCF denominator and repurchase/cancellation cash. A proposal is not payment evidence; later issuer confirmation is still not account-level receipt. This study does not estimate an effect on class prices, investor clientele, credibility or forecast performance. Historical P05 causal status remains untested.

## B. Novo December 2007 basis and conversion case — X03/M02

Use only the five frozen December 3–7 pilot dates and their already delivered forty-date/two-leg comparator table. Extract the ten December daily source records without reexporting prices. Reuse the existing ECB reference series as an explicitly asynchronous daily comparator, with native EUR denominators preserved. No extra dates or selected event windows enter.

Build a dated source-linked action chronology separating document/displayed publication, local/ADR first trading, record, distribution/payment and announced depositary issuance/cancellation suspension clocks. Retain issuer/vendor disagreements and the current retrospective chronology's compressed dates as distinct vintages. Exact event times and independently verified first publication may remain unknown.

For descriptive arithmetic only, compare nominal local B units per raw NYSE ADR under candidate factors one and two. If L is the raw DKK local close, A the raw USD ADR close and F the daily ECB USD-per-DKK reference cross, report candidate ratio `A/(k*L*F)` and discount `100*(1-A/(k*L*F))` for k=1 and k=2, without choosing k by observed prices. Factor two is motivated by separate announced split trading clocks, not certified legal conversion or vendor adjustment semantics. Retain vendor adjustment/action fields; do not apply them silently or splice returns.

These are asynchronous, raw-price/candidate-unit comparators, not corrected fair values, cash-inclusive returns, matched-auction prices or executable conversion opportunities. Exact historical quote venue, raw adjustment semantics, distribution entitlement and actual legal delivery/conversion at each instant remain unqualified. Daily ECB references, later US closes and candidate basis arithmetic never populate synchronized primary slots. All twenty dates, sixty missing primary slots, unknown ages and pre-2005 holds remain unchanged.
