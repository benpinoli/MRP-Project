## Economic hypotheses and rivals

**B01/B02: stable terminal-gap center.** A training-only center can forecast
changes because the forecast is the estimated center minus today's discount.
This is a legitimate change forecast even if the conditional terminal mean is
almost constant. It does not force fair value to zero, establish a structural
equilibrium, or prove that no state information exists.

**M01–M05/X01–X03: measurement versus economic center.** A stable center can
represent real relative-price integration/segmentation, or correction of
transitory quote/FX/basis noise. Both can produce persistence gains. Venue,
ratio, split and synchronized-clock evidence discriminates them. Fees/cash
rights can matter for a payoff without being required to test every observed-
price forecast. Retain local/US ex/trading/record/payment clocks and investor
cash treatment separately.

**Prospective stability.** Historical gains may fail when the center, rights,
source or information-delivery regime changes. The future registry tests that
claim on new information, with native failures and matched benchmark coverage.
Twelve overlapping origins are not twelve independent two-year episodes;
longer horizons require actual elapsed time and more independent evidence.
