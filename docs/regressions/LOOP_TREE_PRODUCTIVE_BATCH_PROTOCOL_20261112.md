## B. Samsung annual delivered payout versus discount changes

Tree P05/P01 and rival S06. Build a retrospective FY2010–FY2024 panel from already held source reports, complete annual discount summaries and reviewed relative non-treasury supply. FY2025 is excluded because complete, definition-reconciled annual payout evidence is not established by the existing partial/current sources. No new generic report acquisition.

Transcription precedence: own fiscal-year report if the class DPS/aggregate cash table is present; otherwise earliest already held report up to two years later containing a clearly identified comparative column. Keep native share units, split normalization, publication unknowns, proposal/payment qualifications and vintage separately. Use only consecutive complete annual pairs; no interpolation, zero-filling, annual-value repetition over daily rows or outcome-driven sample selection. Require at least ten usable annual changes for estimation; otherwise report the failed data gate rather than expanding dates or changing predictors.

Outcome: change in the annual mean of qualified monthly discount observations, in percentage points. Fixed models, all on the same qualified sample:

1. Supply-only: intercept + change in preferred/common non-treasury ratio.
2. Payout-only: intercept + log change in reported aggregate cash dividends.
3. Joint: intercept + those two predictors.
4. Fixed denominator sensitivity: joint model replacing aggregate cash growth by split-normalized common DPS growth.

Report coefficients, ordinary fit, HC1 and fixed HAC1 standard errors/95% intervals and nominal p-values, n/years and source qualifications. No p-value selects another design. The two joint payout terms (aggregate and DPS definitions) are the primary descriptive associations, with their nominal p-values also reported under BH adjustment as one two-test family. All intervals remain fragile in a small observational annual sample. Repurchase cash and policy-defined FCF are not represented by the cash-dividend predictor.

Known-special-year sensitivity: repeat the aggregate joint model excluding outcomes FY2020 and FY2021, which respectively receive the large special allocation and its reversal in growth. Both exclusions are fixed now from source facts; full-sample estimates remain primary. Report all leave-one-year-out joint coefficients as influence diagnostics without selecting a preferred subset. Check whether the preferred-minus-common DPS difference has identifying variation; do not fit a constant class-cash regressor and call it a null mechanism result.

This tests retrospective association with delivered cash, not payout credibility, clientele, policy causality or origin-available predictive features. Unknown original publication dates remain disqualifying for historical forecasting integration, not an automatic bar to a clearly retrospective annual regression.
