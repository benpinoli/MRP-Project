original active exact-class candidate remains on the secondary eligibility
roster. Retired Shell A/B and Roche Genussschein are not reissued or spliced to
successors. Short active histories remain recorded as blocked, not losses.

challenger visible irrespective of the retrospective experiment's winner.

are secondary. Source/mapping, completed-month coverage, matured training,
actual delivery and support gates must pass before each issuance. Missing or
invalid candidates remain explicit. Training uses both label maturity and actual
availability by issuance; nominal dates alone are insufficient.

zero-change persistence at 24 months under pragmatic coverage, with strict
