# Long-horizon independent inspection

## A: pre-run measurement review and historical-source audit

The staged monthly construction averages same-day preferred/common price ratios, retains a complete calendar-month index, applies the declared 10-observation / 80%-of-observed-proxy qualification, and excludes partial October 2026. A quarter requires all three qualified monthly means. The 24/60/120-month series retains endpoint-eligible and strict-complete-window versions, counts missing interior months and reports non-overlap using an explicit anchor. These are descriptive comparisons of heavily overlapping periods; non-overlap anchored at a single date is not a set of independent causal experiments. Inclusive average q uses h+1 monthly values and is not a cash-inclusive holding return.

Review identified that `groupby.last` silently picks an earlier valid quote when the final observed proxy row is missing. Coder corrected the labels to last-valid, saved its date and proxy-row staleness, and added quarter-level endpoint metadata and a synthetic missing-final-row check. This sensitivity is a last-valid proxy quote, not a verified exchange quarter-end close. Monthly thresholds use the EP01 at-least-one-price-eligible session proxy; neither 80% coverage nor the month/quarter completeness label establishes an independent exchange calendar.

Primary issuer history reaches back to 2005: [Samsung annual report 2005](https://www.samsung.com/us/smg/content/dam/samsung/us/aboutsamsung/2017/about-us-sustainability-report-and-policy-annual-report-2005-en.pdf), notes 18 and 23, reports 2005-12-31 issued common 147,299,337 / preferred 22,833,427, and treasury common 16,679,040 / preferred 2,179,693, all pre-split. Issued minus treasury is 130,620,297 / 20,653,734; this is not public float. It does not remove affiliate, strategic, locked or GDR-underlying positions. That report identifies non-cumulative non-voting preferred with additional dividend equal to 1% of par (then KRW5,000), not a 1% increment of common dividend.

[2010 annual report](https://images.samsung.com/is/content/samsung/p5/uk/aboutsamsung/2017/pdf/about-us-sustainability-report-and-policy-annual-report-2010-en.pdf) reports 2010-12-31 treasury common 17,456,260 / preferred 2,979,693. [2010 Q3 statements](https://images.samsung.com/is/content/samsung/assets/global/ir/docs/2010_con_quarter03_all.pdf) show 2010-09-30 issued common 147,299,337 / preferred 22,833,427, and GDR-underlying shares common 8,967,133 / preferred 3,396,390, two GDR certificates per underlying share in that report. Underlying shares are already part of issued shares; do not subtract receipts as additional issued shares. [2016 AGM proxy](https://images.samsung.com/is/content/samsung/assets/global/ir/docs/2016_Proxy_Material.pdf) reports the same class issued totals and 2015-12-31 common treasury 19,902,489. These are sample points, not a continuous annual ownership/float series. Confirm per-report date and first availability; do not forward-fill annual reports as known daily history. Adjust pre-2018 shares for the 50:1 split only for consistent comparison and maintain native counts separately.

A discriminating next mechanism test, conditional on assembling year-end class counts, compares annual issued-minus-treasury relative supply and class turnover per non-treasury share alongside 2/5-year average discount, separating scarcity support from trading capacity deterioration. Neither issued-minus-treasury nor raw volume establishes float, costs, investor identity, or signed flow. Policy choice in 2015 responded to the discount, making reverse causation material. Gaps in 2006–09/2011–14 and post-2015 class counts, annual publication timestamps, affiliate blocks, class-specific institutional and foreign ownership, free-float definitions and comparable true spreads remain unverified pending report extraction. A handful of sources is not proof of continuous coverage.

Vendor counts must not be treated as verified annual supply.

A source-definition trap: the 2010 annual consolidated statement table describes common 129,843,077 / preferred 19,853,734 as shares issued, but these exactly equal the 2010 Q3 legal-issued common 147,299,337 / preferred 22,833,427 less FY2010 treasury 17,456,260 / 2,979,693. Preserve the wording and reconcile legal issued, treasury and non-treasury separately; Q3 legal counts require separate year-end confirmation. The 2015 business report's so-called floating counts common 127,396,848 / preferred 18,547,833 similarly equal legal issued less treasury 19,902,489 / 4,285,594, and do not remove strategic or affiliate holdings. These are non-treasury, not a proven public-float denominator.

## B: annual share panel and pre-run review

The ten additional annual rows (2011–14, 2016–17, 2019, 2021–23) were independently checked against their cited official PDF page tables and issued-minus-treasury arithmetic. For 2013 the voting-share table supplies class issued and treasury counts, while non-treasury values are arithmetic derivations, not verbatim table cells. The same 2013 page gives different affiliated-share counts in narrative and table; do not use either as an unqualified control-block history. Issuer terminology such as “float” is normalized to non-treasury only; it does not exclude strategic/affiliate holdings. No first-publication date has yet been verified, so the panel is retrospective descriptive evidence.

The staged LHB job normalizes pre-split volume and pre-2018 year-end share counts by 50, retains missing years and uses the annual sum of observed price-eligible class volume divided by year-end non-treasury shares. Class volume coverage below 80% is excluded and no missing rows are rescaled. Thus at less than 100% coverage the numerator is an observed-volume lower bound; the denominator is a year-end stock, not annual average float or actual liquidity cost. The ratio can change with coverage and should not be interpreted as investor flow or causal liquidity. A cancellation of already-held treasury shares does not mechanically remove additional non-treasury shares. No fit, iid inference, or forward-fill is warranted by this descriptive construction.
